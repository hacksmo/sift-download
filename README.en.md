# Sift · Downloads

**English** · [中文](README.md)

[Sift](https://sift.hackmo.net/en/) finds photos on your own Mac by describing them in plain language — say "arched doorway, warm light", get the right shot out of thousands in seconds, drag it straight into Final Cut or Finder. The AI runs entirely offline on your machine. Not one photo is uploaded.

This repository holds **only the installer**. The source and the marketing site live elsewhere.

## Download

Click **Releases** on the right → the newest `Sift-x.y.z.dmg`.

Or go straight to the latest one: <https://github.com/hacksmo/sift-download/releases/latest>

## Install

1. Download `Sift-x.y.z.dmg` and open it.
2. Drag `Sift.app` into your Applications folder.
3. **macOS will block it the first time.** The app is signed ad-hoc and not yet notarized by Apple. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, then click **Open Anyway** at the bottom of the page. Or right-click the app icon and choose **Open**. You only do this once; normal double-click launching works after that.
4. On first launch Sift offers to import the semantic model (~720 MB, optional). Skip it and Sift falls back to matching on filenames and PDF text — still useful, just not semantic.

## Requirements

- Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later)
- macOS 14 Sonoma or newer
- Intel Macs, Windows and Linux are **not** supported

## Privacy

The index, the vectors and the thumbnails all stay in your own Application Support folder. No account, no cloud sync, no upload of any kind. It works with the network unplugged.
